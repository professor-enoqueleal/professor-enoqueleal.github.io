### About the professor

Enoque Leal is a software developer with over ten years of experience in the information technology industry, working in medium and large-sized companies in mission-critical environments.

In addition to his role in the information technology industry, he also works as an undergraduate professor at Centro Universitario Senac and a postgraduate professor at Unyleya Educacional.

### About the labs

These self-guided labs **(guides)** aim to help students enrolled in the **Systems Analysis and Development** technology program, specifically those taking the **Integrative Project III** course. Throughout the labs, it will be demonstrated how to create a Java Web application using Java and Maven, persisting data in a relational database (H2 DB).

All of this will be done through a self-guided lab model, allowing students to revisit the content at any time, facilitating the learning process.

All lab content is also available on YouTube in video lesson format. Therefore, students have more than one option to consume the content.

Any questions related to the lab content can be written in the comments at the end of each lab for later correction.

Feedback and constructive criticism are also welcome.
